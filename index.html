How to use this

Getting around
Every section has its own address — copy the URL from the address bar and you will land back on that exact section. Back and Forward behave normally. Press / anywhere to search either the current page or the whole site. Everything is reachable by keyboard alone.
What is included
Content follows the course's stated learning objectives and is organized around the distinctions, laboratory patterns, mechanisms, and management principles most likely to matter on the quiz.
The color system
Blue is normal physiology and signaling. Red is excess or acute danger. Teal is treatment. Amber is a diagnostic clue or a caution. Purple is a structural lesion. A gray dashed line means inhibition or a lost signal. Color is never the only cue — every one is also labeled in words.
What this is not
Not an official course product, not a required module, and not a clinical decision-support tool. It is one student's synthesis of open-access clinical references, organized around the course's stated objectives. It is a student synthesis and should be checked against the course materials if anything appears inconsistent.
